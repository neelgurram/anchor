What Anchor can’t do yet.

Practice clues

Our clues are made around real expert-marked spots. Reading real ultrasound images isn’t built yet.

Display brain

The 3D brain on screen is a standard model for show. Every accuracy number comes from the real patients.

Small and slow

At most 16 qubits, and the quantum computer took hours to reply. Surgery needs an answer in a second.

Not hospital-ready

Real use needs FDA approval, and patient scans can’t go to an outside computer without review.
