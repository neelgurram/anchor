09 · Limitations

What Anchor can’t do yet.

Simulated matcher

Matches are generated around real expert landmarks; reading real ultrasound images isn’t built.

Display brain

The 3D brain is a template for display; every error is measured on the real patient’s points.

Small and slow

At most 16 qubits, and cloud queues took hours, while surgery needs answers in a second.

Not clinical

Real use needs FDA clearance, and patient data can’t go to a cloud computer without review.
