5 · What’s next

What’s new, and what’s missing.

New

Real patients, graded in millimetres

Most quantum projects use made-up data. Anchor is tested on real brain-surgery patients.

A map that checks itself

It warns when its own fix is bad, without being told the right answer.

Honest by design

The rules were written before any quantum run, and every result is kept, even where normal computers won.

Missing

Practice matches

Matches are built around expert-marked spots. Reading real ultrasound images isn’t built yet.

Small and slow

At most 16 qubits, and the IBM computer took hours to reply. Surgery needs an answer in a second.

Not hospital-ready

It would need FDA approval and a privacy review. The 3D brain on screen is a standard model for display.
