05 · Results: the self-check

Anchor warns the surgeon when its own fix is bad.

Anchor tests itself: it hides one clue at a time and checks whether the others can still point to where it should be. If they can’t, something is wrong, and it sounds an alarm. We tuned it on half the patients and graded it on the other half.
