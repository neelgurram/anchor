03 · Why quantum, honestly

Quantum fits the shape. Today, classical still wins.

Why it fits

QAOA holds every possible selection at once and tilts the odds toward the best one; we sample it and keep the best answer. Max clique is NP-hard, so it grows hard fast as cases get bigger.

Honest limits

At our size, an exact classical solver takes 1 millisecond, and today’s noise limits us to small circuits. We claim no speed-up and no quantum advantage.
