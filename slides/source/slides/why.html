03 · Why quantum, honestly

Quantum fits this puzzle, but it isn’t faster yet.

Why it fits

A quantum computer can weigh many possible answers at once and lean toward the best ones. Puzzles like ours get much harder as they grow, which is where future quantum computers could help.

The honest part

Today, a normal computer solves our size in a thousandth of a second, and quantum chips still make mistakes. So we don’t claim quantum is faster, only that it reaches the same answer.
