It runs on a real IBM quantum computer.

We ran 48 puzzles on IBM’s ibm_fez chip. In 42 of them the chip’s best answer was the perfect one, and the fixed map was as good as a normal computer’s (2.3 vs 2.2 mm).

The real chip (green) got 83% of the valid answers our noisy-chip prediction (orange) expected and 6× more than random guessing (grey), but fewer as puzzles and circuits got bigger. The chip really solves the puzzle; noise is what limits it today.
