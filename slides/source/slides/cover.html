Anchor

Keeping a brain surgeon’s map accurate when the brain moves.

A quantum computing project, tested on 22 real patients.

Neel Gurram

Introduce yourself: Neel Gurram, solo project. One-line pitch: during brain surgery the brain moves, so the surgeon’s MRI map goes wrong; Anchor works out which ultrasound matches to trust, fixes the map, and warns when the fix can’t be trusted. The picture is Anchor’s fixed map, coloured by how much to trust each area.
