07 · When would quantum win?

Hardware must get about 8× cleaner first.

A real surgical case needs 80–200 qubits. At today’s two-qubit error rate, a clean 30-qubit run happens 0.3% of the time.

Error must fall from 3.3 × 10⁻³ to about 4 × 10⁻⁴, or to error-corrected qubits for full cases, with answers in under a second.
