Conclusions: the noise model is a fair, slightly optimistic guide. Depth is what kills a circuit: the deep anneal that won on the perfect simulator collapsed on hardware, as the noise study predicted. Error suppression (XY4 dynamical decoupling + twirling) did not help, but the two jobs ran about three hours apart, so calibration drift is part of that gap. In the 6 runs that missed, 5 maps came out worse. Two patient cases at four sizes on one night is a small sample.
