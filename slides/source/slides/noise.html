Exact numbers: the chip got 83% of the valid answers our noise model predicted, so the model is a fair, slightly optimistic guide. At 12 qubits the deepest circuit kept 4–13% valid answers vs 22–33% for the shortest. With error suppression the chip got 85% as many valid answers as without; the two jobs ran about three hours apart, so calibration drift is part of that gap. Map error 2.28 vs 2.21 mm (exact solver), 12.6 mm uncorrected. In the 6 runs that missed, 5 maps came out worse. Two patient cases on one night is a small sample.
