01 · The problem

The surgeon’s map goes out of date mid-operation.

Brain-tumour surgeons steer with a 3D map made from an MRI taken before surgery.

Once the skull opens, the brain sags, so the map no longer matches the patient. A surgeon on a stale map can leave tumour behind or cut healthy tissue.

Who it affects: neurosurgeons, their patients, and hospitals that can’t afford an operating-room MRI.
