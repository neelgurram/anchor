Then Anchor fixes the map and checks it.

Fix. Anchor bends the MRI so each landmark lands on its kept match, and the brain between them moves smoothly along.

Check. It hides one kept match at a time and asks whether the others still point to the right place.

Where they don’t, the map turns red. If too much is red, an alarm tells the surgeon not to trust the update.
