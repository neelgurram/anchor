01 · Why it’s hard

One wrong match bends the whole map.

An operating-room MRI fixes the map, but it costs millions and few hospitals have one.

Ultrasound is cheap and already in the room: match landmarks between the two scans, then warp the map.

But ultrasound is noisy. Some matches are wrong, and each wrong one drags the map with it.

Trusting every top match: still 16.5 mm off, and the alarm fires.
