01 · Why it’s hard to fix

Fixing the map means trusting the right clues.

During surgery, doctors can take an ultrasound: a quick, live scan. They match spots on it to the same spots on the old MRI, to see how far everything moved.

But ultrasound is blurry, so some matches are wrong. If even one wrong match is used, it pulls the whole corrected map out of shape.

What happens if you trust every match: one wrong clue (red line) bends the map, and it stays 16.5 mm off. Red marks where the map is wrong.
