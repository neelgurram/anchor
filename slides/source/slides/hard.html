01 · Why it’s hard to fix

Fixing the map means trusting the right clues.

During surgery, doctors can take an ultrasound: a quick, live scan. They match spots on it to the same spots on the old MRI, to see how far everything moved.

But ultrasound is blurry, so some matches are wrong. One wrong match pulls the whole map out of shape, like one tent peg in the wrong place pulls the whole tent crooked.

What happens if you trust every match: the map is still 16.5 mm off. Red marks where it can’t be trusted, and the app warns the surgeon.
