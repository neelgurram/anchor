03 · Turning it into a quantum puzzle

It’s like finding a group of friends at a party.

At a party, find the biggest group where everyone knows everyone else. Our clues work the same way: we want the biggest group where every clue agrees with every other clue.

We turn that into a scoring game: keeping a clue earns a point, keeping two clues that disagree costs points. A quantum computer searches for the best score.

1 clue = 1 qubit

A qubit is a quantum on/off switch: keep the clue, or drop it.

Best score wins

The best score is always the biggest group of clues that all agree.

12 qubits

for the demo patient: 6 spots, 2 possible matches each

Scientists call this a maximum-clique problem, written as a QUBO
