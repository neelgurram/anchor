SAY: Choosing which matches to keep is a puzzle, and it fits a quantum computer. Each match is a qubit: keep or drop. In superposition the qubits hold every possible group at once. We measure thousands of times and keep the best group. To be honest, today a normal computer is just as good. — IF ASKED: For judges: this is a maximum-clique problem written as a QUBO: minimise −Σxᵢ + P·Σxᵢxⱼ over pairs that disagree, with P = 2 so breaking a rule never pays. A test checks every small case by brute force to prove the best score is always the true biggest group. Max clique is NP-hard in general. Quantum ideas used: superposition (all selections at once), interference steered by the circuit settings, and sampling.
