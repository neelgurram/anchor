03 · The quantum shape

“Which matches agree?” is a max-clique problem.

Think of a party: find the biggest group of guests who all know each other.

Here each possible match is one qubit, and every pair of matches that disagree gets a penalty. The lowest-energy answer is the largest group that all agree, written as a QUBO that quantum hardware runs natively.

1 match = 1 qubit

1 means trust it, 0 means drop it

−Σ xᵢ + P·Σ xᵢxⱼ

reward each match kept; penalty P for every disagreeing pair kept

6–16 qubits

per case in our tests; 12 in the demo

Rubric 02. A clique is a group where everyone is connected. Two matches are “connected” when the distance between them in the MRI equals the distance in the ultrasound, within a tolerance we fixed before any quantum run. P = 2 so breaking a rule never pays; a test brute-forces every small case to prove the QUBO’s best answer is the true max clique.
