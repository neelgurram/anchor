2 · Our solution

Correct matches agree with each other.

The brain shifts as one piece, so spots that were 3 cm apart on the MRI are still about 3 cm apart on the ultrasound.

A wrong match breaks that pattern.

So Anchor keeps the biggest group of matches that all agree with each other, and throws out the rest.
