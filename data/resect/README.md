# RESECT landmark files

These are the 22 `CaseN-MRI-beforeUS.tag` files from the RESECT database. Each one lists about 15 expert landmark pairs between the pre-operative MRI and the intra-operative ultrasound taken before resection, in world coordinates (mm).

- **Source:** Xiao Y., Fortin M., Unsgård G., Rivaz H., Reinertsen I. "RESECT: a clinical database of pre-operative MRI and intra-operative ultrasound in low-grade glioma surgeries." *Medical Physics* 44(7), 2017. DOI of the data: 10.11582/2017.00004
